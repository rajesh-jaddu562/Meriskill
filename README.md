⭐ Google Ads Campaign Analysis — Podha

A marketing analytics project evaluating Podha’s digital advertising performance, with a focus on Google Ads.
The goal was to understand customer acquisition, revenue contribution, and identify the most effective advertising channel.

📊 Project Summary


* $24.89M revenue from Google Ads

* 56K orders and 17.31K customers acquired

* $445.20 AOV — highest among all channels

* Google Ads contributes ~80% of total revenue and delivers the lowest CAC

🛠 Tools Used
Python     · SQL    · Power BI    · Gamma App

🔄 What I Did

* Cleaned and explored ad performance data

* Compared Google Ads, Meta Ads, YouTube & Other channels

* Analyzed revenue per customer, CAC, and product performance

* Built a Power BI dashboard

* Delivered final insights as a presentation

💡 Key Insights

* Google Ads is the top-performing channel across all metrics

* Plants generate 96% of total revenue

* Best-selling products include large potted plants, plant soil, plant food & ceramic pots

🎯 Recommendations

* Increase budget for Google Ads to maximize ROI

* Optimize keywords, ad copy, bidding & landing pages

* Use Meta & YouTube mainly for branding and diversification
